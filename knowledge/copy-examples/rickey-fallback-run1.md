### 5 · Break their current approach

## What Keeps Beginners at Zero Sales on TikTok Shop

**Watching other people's wins at night.**
Their videos don't post one for you. Every night of watching is another night with nothing posted on your account.

**Jumping between free tutorials.**
One says post 5 to 10 times a day. The next one says something else. One beginner said it "led to confusion and poor results."
A lot of that advice is old. The 2026 rules: 1,000 followers to sell, and about 3 shoppable videos a day while you're under 5,000.

**Posting a burst of videos with no plan, then stopping.**
"I've been posting for months and have sold exactly 0."
A few hundred views is normal for a shop video. Most first sales land in the first few weeks, not the first few days. Stop in week one, and you stop before most first sales land.

**Waiting on free samples before you film.**
"I NEVER receive responses."
Start with what you already own. Then TikTok's refundable samples. Free samples come later, once you've made a sale.

You've been taught to post as much as you can and wait for views, when you actually need one planned video a day, for a product that already sells, for 30 days straight.
