# House style: what every page gets from its first build

These are the defaults David's feedback produced on the Rickey page (12 rounds, Nov 20 2026). Build them in from the first draft so the client never has to ask for them again. The exact values are the ones that passed review; keep them unless the brand needs otherwise. Example values use Rickey's brand (red `#D30805`, black `#101017`); swap in your brand's colors.

Why each rule exists, and how it was found: `knowledge/feedback-log.md`. Code for each component: `knowledge/components/CATALOGUE.md` and `examples/rickey-headstart/body-src.html`.

**Layout and flow**
1. **Mobile first.** Build and check at 393×852 first, then 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1920×1080. Phone CSS goes last, in `@media (max-width:820px)`; desktop CSS in `@media (min-width:900px)`. Phone section padding is `46px 0`.
2. **Alert bar at the very top.** A brand gradient (`linear-gradient(90deg, <brand-black> 0%, <dark shade of brand color> 52%, <brand color> 100%)`), head font 800, uppercase, 12.5px on phones and 13px on desktop, letter-spacing .07-.09em, an optional blinking dot (1.4s). Text is the offer one-liner, e.g. "One-time $27 · instant access".
3. **Hero above the fold on 393×852** (use the one-line/horizontal logo in the hero: a stacked logo at 150px costs ~30px and pushed the dry run's button to 791): alert bar + logo (150px) + proof pill (13.5px) + H1 (30px/1.12) + hero image + subheadline (17px/1.55, color `#24242E`) + full-width button (16px 14px padding, 15px text) + fine print (13.5px). Target: fine print bottom ≤ ~800px of 852 (this page: btn 764, fine 795). Readability wins over squeezing: David rejected 10.5-15px text.
4. **Hero image first in the DOM** (`fetchpriority="high"`, no lazy loading) and placed under the H1 with flex `order`. Hero elements are not reveal-animated (the kit forces `.hero .r{opacity:1}`).
5. **Desktop hero in two columns.** `grid-template-columns:.82fr 1.18fr`, areas `"logo logo" "pill img" "h1 img" "lede img" "cta img"`, gap 44px, max-width 1300px, left-aligned text, H1 `clamp(38px,3.4vw,50px)`, lede 18px/1.55, button `20px 40px`/17px, image ~700px wide at 1280. **The logo strip under the hero must also show on the first screen** at 1280×800 and 1440×900.
6. **Transparent-background hero mockup**, so it sits on the hero gradient. Reuse it in the final close.
7. **Subheadline:** highlighter on the proof phrase, bold brand color on the 2-4 outcome verbs, nothing on process details. Keep it short on phones by cutting redundant nouns ("a 7-day course").
8. **One CTA label everywhere:** `GET INSTANT ACCESS · $<price> →`. Fill it from one constant (`@@CTA@@`) and count the buttons on each build.
9. **CTA routing:** every button `href="#buy"` (the offer stack); only the offer stack button links to checkout, so there's one placeholder `PASTE-YOUR-CHECKOUT-LINK`. Smooth scroll comes from `html{scroll-behavior:smooth}`.
10. **Sticky CTA bar** that appears after the hero and hides while ≥15% of the offer stack is visible. Dark `rgba(16,16,23,.97)`, 2px brand-red top border, label "Instant access · **$27**" plus the same button, safe-area padding, `footer{padding-bottom:110px}`.
11. **Brand/press logo slider right under the hero** ("Brands <Creator> has worked with"): grayscale logos, phone logos 40px tall at opacity .75, edges faded with a mask, and it **never runs out** (C1).
12. **Dark/light rhythm:** dark sections at 2, 5, 8, 11 alternating with white, sand (`#F4F2EF`) and paper, so the page never reads as one long section.
13. **Breathing room:** statement lines 1.15-1.25em apart, about 40-46px between a slider and the next text block. Nothing should feel cramped on phones.
14. **A visual for every outcome claim:** real screenshots for proof claims, an AI mockup for product features. Outcome cards have a number chip, a real heading (19px phone / 29px desktop) and text (21px desktop), with the image on the right on desktop.
15. **Pain cards** (the "watching other people win" style): no 01/02/03 numbers. Use an icon tile, a bold lead line (20-22px), muted body (17-18px) and a punchline in the light brand color.
16. **Founder story section:** on phones the headline comes first, then the photo(s) and the logo slider, then the story. Each beat gets its own look (lead / aside / turn / crossed-out-belief box / moment with a red left rule / gem in handwriting). Photos must be **real** (never AI) and licensed. With two photos: side by side on phones, stacked and overlapping on desktop. The photo column isn't sticky if it's taller than the screen. **The story text is never rewritten** without explicit approval.
17. **Offer stack (JJ structure, `id="buy"`):** a dark header band (title plus a red pill "One-time $X · instant access"), a product mockup, item rows (thumbnail · name · crossed-out value on the right), a "Total value: ~~$N~~" bar, and a red-bordered deal box with a "You save $M" badge on its top edge, "Today: just **$X.**" (84px desktop / 70px phone), "No subscription. No upsell required.", the full-width checkout button and a trust row (One-Time Payment · Instant Access · N-Day Refund). **Never show the order bump on the sales page** (David, later round: it lives only in the checkout; an earlier version had a $17 add-on box here). Write "value", never "regular price". **No fake urgency** ("Today only" only if a real deadline exists).
18. **The guarantee is its own block** right after the offer: a seal SVG (number + DAYS + FULL REFUND), "Your Money's **Protected**", and the one-sentence condition.
19. **Only list what's really included.** Recompute the total value and the savings whenever an item changes, and check each item against the client's latest list.
20. **Video carousel** of the creator's clips in an early dark section: muted, looping, `playsinline`, lazy-loaded from a CDN with light transforms, paused off screen, paused on hover.
21. **Proof wall slider** of real screenshots, with the note "Those are my results… Not a promise of what you'll make."
22. **No placeholders at go-live.** Drop a spot whose asset is missing (sections can run text only).
23. **Footer:** brand logo, earnings disclaimer, platform trademark/non-affiliation notice, refund policy, ©, and an oversized gradient wordmark.
24. **No sideways scroll at any size** (`scrollWidth === innerWidth`). Put `min-width:0` on grid children that contain `width:max-content` tracks.
25. **Motion and accessibility:** `prefers-reduced-motion` stops every animation and makes sliders scrollable (`overflow-x:auto`). Cloned slider items get `aria-hidden="true"`. The sticky bar is `aria-hidden` and its button `tabindex="-1"`, since it duplicates other buttons.
26. **Desktop type floor:** body 17.5-21px, offer list 17.5px, statements 24px.
27. **Process for each round:** back up `body-src`/`copy`/`index` as `-vN` before editing, patch with assert-guarded replacements, rebuild and run the copy check, review on phone and desktop, append the round's decisions to `learned-rules.md`, then reopen the page for the client.
28. **Speed before go-live:** WebP at display size (`tools/optimize_images.py`), hero with `srcset` + `fetchpriority="high"`, everything else lazy, videos via the light Cloudinary transform. Target ≤ ~1.5 MB of images on a phone full scroll (`stages/8-speed.md`).
29. **Checkout, upsell and thank-you pages** are built in Part 2 (`stages/funnel-1-plan.md`). Upsell pages follow the same house style plus the upsell structure in `knowledge/upsell-and-bump.md` (confirmation alert bar, "you just unlocked…" headline, crossed-value stack, one-time-offer line, YES button + plain NO link).
