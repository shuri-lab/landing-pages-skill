## 3. Why this offer
- The mechanism / method (how it works, in plain words):
  - 1,000 followers first: post regular videos about yourself on one topic that feels natural, 3 to 5 a day; you are building a brand "unknowingly". Then switch to products that fit that topic (his brother went from tech talk to tech products) [Rickey's answers; Course deck, lesson 2]
  - Check the product before you film: lots of sales and few creators, "demand with not enough supply. That's a gold mine."; a few dollars per sale; you can show it in 2 seconds [Rickey's answers; Course deck, lesson 3; Starter list]
  - Show, don't tell: grab them in the first 2 seconds, show the product doing its thing, give one reason to want it, say "tap the orange cart" [Course deck, lessons 4 and 5]
  - Use what makes you you, even an insecurity: "Use your unfair advantage." (his gut cleanse video) [Rickey's answers]
  - Check sales daily and remake what sells: "You keep bleeding it until it's gone." [Rickey's answers]
  - Keep the account safe: no dupes or look-alikes, no weight-loss claims, always show the product you promote [Rickey's answers; Course deck, lesson 6]
  - The challenge makes it a habit: one video a day; "Miss a day? You do not start over, you just post today." [30-day challenge]
- What becomes easier, faster or better:
  - A set task every day for 30 days, instead of opening TikTok and closing it with nothing posted [30-day challenge; Tool output v1]
  - Pick products that already sell before filming anything [Course deck, lesson 3; Rickey's answers]
  - "No store, no inventory, no shipping. You just make videos." [Course deck, lesson 1]
  - Start with the phone you have and things already in your house [Course deck, lesson 4; Rickey's answers]
  - A simple video in about 10 to 15 minutes; a week batched in one sitting [Rickey's answers; 30-day challenge]
  - Progress you can see early: the streak, weekly check-ins, 1,000 followers, first sale [30-day challenge]
  - Fewer violations: the rules Rickey learned the hard way [Course deck, lesson 6; Rickey's answers]
- How it differs from what competitors promise:
  - Competitors sell income ("$10K/month", "passive" income, "no face, no talking, no following"); Headstart promises a first sale and a habit, with no income figure [Competitors; Market]
  - Built on the 2026 rules (1,000 followers to sell, 3 shoppable videos a day under 5,000) while most advice still says "post 5 to 10 times a day" [Competitors; Market]
  - Low-ticket courses leave people alone in the first 1 to 3 weeks, where most quit; this is a 30-day daily plan plus a channel [Competitors]
  - $27 one-time vs $15 to $60 a month for most communities and $497 to $4,000 for coaching [Competitors]
  - Taught by someone who sells, against "The only people making $10k are the ones selling the courses." [Customer research]
