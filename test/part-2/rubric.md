# Part 2 rubric (100 points)

**Hard fails (score 0):** the bump is named on the sales page plan, in the main email, or delivered with the main course · a fake deadline · an invented number, value, testimonial or guarantee · a paid file linked on the thank-you page · sideways scroll on a phone · a leftover from the Rickey example.

| Points | Check |
|---|---|
| 15 | `build_funnel_pages.py` 0 to fix; `check_funnel_pages.mjs` all pass (automatic) |
| 10 | Right bump and upsell chosen, reasons tie to the tests in `funnel-offers.md` |
| 10 | Asked the client the right questions (values, guarantee on the upsell, the 30+ count, 10x, the deadline) |
| 15 | Bump: headline, OTO line, one pain + one real number + one promise, ~130 characters, pops on the phone |
| 25 | Upsell: alert bar, headline, sub, bridge, 3 result-first bullets, stack with "so you…" lines, correct total and savings, price, the right guarantee, YES, NO. Reads like the founder |
| 10 | Checkout: form on the first phone screen, left column complete, approved guarantee word for word |
| 10 | Thank-you and emails: one action first, one email per product, no leaks |
| 5 | Brand applied, light accent handled (readable text on yellow), both sizes looked at in `review.md` |
